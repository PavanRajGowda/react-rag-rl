Metric definitions and aggregation
Training reward curves use changing training questions and are not held-out evaluation. Accuracy uses fresh accepted rollout episodes; infrastructure failures stay in audit logs and do not become zero rewards. Percentages combine numerators/denominators. Loss and policy metrics describe pre-update forwards; fixed-reference KL describes post-update weights. Sample-based KL estimates may be negative; they are not full reference KL.
